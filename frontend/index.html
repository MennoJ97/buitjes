About this map

Rainfall intensity in 5-minute steps, six hours ahead. KNMI produces it by blending radar echoes with the HARMONIE-AROME weather model, so the near term is driven by what the radar can see and the later hours by the model. Accuracy drops the further right you scrub.

Nowcast
First two hours, mostly radar extrapolation.
Forecast
Beyond that, increasingly the weather model.

The blend is seamless — the split above is a reading aid, not a change of data source. Each frame is the median of 20 ensemble members.

Click anywhere on the map to read the rain rate and the full timeline for that spot. Space plays, ← → step frame by frame.
